VELVET · CHARACTERS

Pack your characters.

Browsers do not let a page read loose files from your disk. When you open the game by double-clicking index.html, it reads the characters from one packed file, characters/characters-pack.js. Run this after you add or change a model or texture.

  1. Edit characters/characters.js and put your .glb and image files in the characters folder.
  2. Click below and choose the VELVET game folder (the one with index.html).
  3. Reload the game.
