FULL follows the cue ball's real path off the cushions to the first ball it touches: a green ring means a legal contact, red means a foul (or an in-off), a small ring shows where the white would stop short. Ideal for escaping snookers. While the bot lines up, its planned path is shown in gold. SHORT shows only the first stretch; OFF shows only the cue.

SNOOKER 101

Pot a red (1 point), then nominate and pot a colour (2–7 points). Colours return to their spots while reds remain. Once the last red and its following colour are played, clear colours in order: yellow, green, brown, blue, pink, black. Wrong first contact, illegal pots, in-offs and push shots are fouls (4 points minimum). After a foul your opponent chooses to play on or put you back in; after a foul and a miss they may also have the balls replaced (three misses with a clear line lose the frame). If a foul leaves you snookered you get a free ball. Scores level at the end means a re-spotted black.
