HOW TO PLAY.

MOUSE MOVEPoint on the playing surface to aim the cue. In the AIM view, moving the mouse sideways over the table turns the aim; with ⚙ AIM · HOLD THE BUTTON, only while the left button is held.
CLICK COLOURAfter potting a red, point your cue at a coloured ball: the first object ball in the aim line is automatically nominated. You may still click a colour directly to override that choice. Reds are not selectable.
DRAG WHITEAt the start of a frame or after an in-off, move the cue ball inside the semicircular D.
AUTO CAMEnables automatic camera direction; the arrows and TOP provide instant manual views.
PICK FOCUS / FClick PICK FOCUS then select any point on the felt, or Shift + click directly to refocus. CUE BALL and RESET quickly change focus.
RAIL FADE / VFront wooden rails gradually become transparent when they hide a ball. Toggle to see fully solid rails instead.
ARENAReferee and spectators respond to the real result and difficulty of a shot. Routine pots stay quiet; difficult pots earn a standing reaction.
HPin / unpin the score panels; otherwise they hide automatically.
CAMERA VIEWSAIM (behind the cue ball, P), TOP (the whole table), TV (broadcast angles, ◀ ▶) and FREE. C goes through them, Home resets the view, ⚙ holds the camera settings: sensitivity, smoothing, the length of a change of view, inverted vertical and AUTO CAM (a broadcast angle while the balls run, back to your view after; A).
RIGHT DRAGMoves the camera (in the AIM view it raises it or swings it round the cue ball; the aim never moves). With FREE, a left drag turns it; the middle button or Alt + drag turns it in any view; Ctrl / ⌘ + drag also moves it. Shift makes any of it fine. Double-click a ball to focus on it.
SPEAKERClick the speaker in the top bar to hear a test impact and enable or mute the audio.
WHEELZoom (closer or wider in the AIM view, to the pointer from above). Over the shot power, the wheel sets the power; ↑ / ↓ too.TOUCHOne finger aims (a tap on a ball aims at it; with FREE it turns the camera), a pinch zooms, two fingers move the camera. Slide the FINE AIM strip for small turns and pull the cue at the side down for the power: let go to strike, slide it back to cancel.

GAiming guide. FULL follows the cue ball's real path off the cushions to the first ball it touches: a green ring means a legal contact, red means a foul (or an in-off), a small ring shows where the white would stop short. Ideal for escaping snookers. While the bot lines up, its planned path is shown in gold. SHORT shows only the first stretch; OFF shows only the cue.

SNOOKER 101

Pot a red (1 point), then nominate and pot a colour (2–7 points). Colours return to their spots while reds remain. Once the last red and its following colour are played, clear colours in order: yellow, green, brown, blue, pink, black. Wrong first contact, illegal pots, in-offs and push shots are fouls (4 points minimum). After a foul your opponent chooses to play on or put you back in; after a foul and a miss they may also have the balls replaced (three misses with a clear line lose the frame). If a foul leaves you snookered you get a free ball. Scores level at the end means a re-spotted black.
